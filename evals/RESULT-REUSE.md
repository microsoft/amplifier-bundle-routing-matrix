# Reusable evidence and safe scenarios (DRAFT 2026-10-02)

Continuation design for the maintained routing evaluation program, **not additions to its implemented v1 schema**. Read [README](README.md), [DESIGN](DESIGN.md), [REVIEW](REVIEW.md), then scoped [AGENTS](AGENTS.md). Routing owns its evaluation configurations, scenarios, graders, reuse rules and promotion policy; generic execution bricks remain in the separate evaluation library as optional development dependencies. Current code still implements offline planning/readiness/analysis only. Future reuse fields, acquisition adapters, telemetry, execution and publication require tests and live calibration before use.

## 1. Two recurring workloads

**Targeted refresh:** one new model/configuration against incumbent for affected roles on a qualified frozen core plus a small untouched rotating portion. Reuse privately validated unchanged historical cells for context; obtain a contemporaneous incumbent anchor because providers/runtime can drift. Predeclare symmetric pairing, replication/escalation rules and the whole budget. Output regressions/uncertainty, not automatic matrix rankings.

**Recalibration:** task ceiling/floor or leakage; changed suite/grader/environment; new roles/modalities/matrix; changed bundle/provider/module/tool/delegation/context/dispatch semantics. Requalify affected conditions, broaden independent families and preregister confirmation endpoints, margins, weights and power. Bridge old/new conditions; simultaneous model/runtime changes need controlled attribution or an explicit inability to separate effects.

This is not a paid recurrence schedule. A tiny pack screens major regressions, not small definitive ranking differences. Repeats measure stochasticity within tasks; they are not independent task families. Old catalog/inference smokes are compatibility evidence, not retained quality baselines.

## 2. Evidence planes and fingerprints

Keep subject, assessment and accounting conditions distinct:

| Plane | Private authoritative record | Public permitted class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | Delivered model revision, exact connection binding, runtime/source/matrix/override/context/tool/fixture identity, actual request tree and budget | Allowlisted public code/task/matrix/native-setting revisions, declared limitations |
| Assessment | Rubric/reference/judge versions, complete criterion receipts, disagreement, artifact sufficiency | Public rubric version and sanitized aggregates or synthetic/public-fixture results |
| Accounting | Call-ID deduplication across descendants/attempts, measured/unknown usage, rates/currency/cache/reasoning-token semantics | Approved totals/coverage, cited public price revision; no connection identity |

A private full envelope may use hashes of protected contents internally under local access control. Its private lookup ID should be random and opaque, with the mapping kept private. **Never export that private ID or protected hashes.** A public release mints a separate evidence ID after review. Public `subject_class` is weaker equivalence, not a full fingerprint; equality cannot prove exact-comparable runs.

The current normalizer accepts its strict existing schema only. Do not add envelope fields to v1 receipts and assume they are validated; future adapters must version the schema and test the trust boundary. A manifest hash proves input consistency, not delivered models, account identity or truth of grading assertions. The current sample is synthetic and deliberately non-promotional.

Comparability uses a preregistered projection of **assigned conditions**, excluding the treatment. That projection covers task/fixture versions, environment, tools, fixed root/controller and protocol, context/native settings, budgets and grader conditions. Observed call trees, chosen tool sequences, success/failure, latency and cost remain outcomes and may legitimately differ because of the treatment. Do not require those observations to match or filter reusable runs because their score is poor.

Delivered model/binding and trace completeness establish treatment fidelity and measurement validity. Wrong-target or protocol violations are separate dispositions, with all scheduled cells retained for whole-policy/intention-to-treat reporting. Per-protocol comparisons must declare exclusion rules and report excluded counts/missingness; they must not quietly replace the assigned comparison.

Exact-comparable reuse needs delivered-model revision/generation evidence and unchanged relevant binding identity; alias-only or hidden served revisions cannot establish that assurance. Keep those results historical/uncertain and label any contemporaneous comparison's delivery limits. Strategy semantic generations and actual provider model revisions are distinct identities.

Predeclare the fresh incumbent-anchor diagnostic and historical-variability reference for each family. Material disagreement, unavailable identity or insufficient observations invalidate confident historical reuse for that decision; downgrade to historical and obtain symmetric fresh paired evidence or report uncertainty. Do not move the anchor threshold after observing a desired result.

## 3. Reuse dispositions

| Disposition | Minimum checks | Safe interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact-comparable | Equal preregistered assigned conditions except treatment; qualified setup/measurement; validated delivered identity; matched tasks/budgets | Paired comparison with potentially different behavior, call trees, failures and costs retained |
| Regradable | Retained artifacts/reference access sufficient; both/all relevant arms scored under the same new rubric; old rubric/results retained | New assessment of old output, not new execution or latency |
| Accounting-only | Sufficient itemized usage and currency/units/rate provenance; all attempt/controller/subject/application components retained | Repricing estimate or corrected accounting, not fresh spend |
| Historical/context-only | Changed or uncertain task/runtime/role exposure/provider identity | Context for choosing a new experiment, not current promotion evidence |
| Invalid/unverifiable | Missing/corrupt traces, grades, coverage or identity; unqualified task | Audit/fault evidence, not a zero-filled loss or apparent winner |

Readiness and endpoint eligibility remain independent. A verified subject timeout is failed completion even when criterion grades are unavailable. Invalid assessment cannot establish critical pass/fail. Unknown critical status does not erase an already failed necessary success criterion. Unknown price blocks USD comparisons, not otherwise valid quality assessment. Cleanup failure blocks operational readiness without erasing observed task output.

Keep every scheduled cell, immutable attempt and cost. Multi-attempt cells stay blocked in this starter; a later retry policy must be preregistered and account for all attempts. Never cherry-pick best attempts, selectively retry losing arms, or merge differing model snapshots under a display alias. Public reports state their private-assurance/reproduction limits rather than pretending public class hashes prove full parity.

## 4. From failure mechanisms to synthetic tasks

Private records may identify mechanisms worth testing, but their text/identities are not a reusable public dataset. Do not replace tokens in actual captures and call them synthetic.

Treat reconstruction as a clean-room authoring boundary: privately distill the causal requirement, uncertainty and difficult state/edge cases; pass only that abstract specification to fixture generation. Rebuild domain, organization structure, content, timelines, distributions, identifiers, artifacts and independently computed references. Do not retain recognizable business architecture or a unique combination of non-PII details. Keep any source mapping private.

The distiller and author must be separate contexts: the fixture author has never received the original private records or inherited a conversation containing them. An authorized privacy reviewer clears the abstract handoff before authoring, then a separate stranger/nonrecognition and rights check clears the completed artifact. Keep private revision-bound decision receipts; only a separate approved release reference may be public. Unknown clearance fails closed. Escalate uncertain recognizability/rights or policy exceptions to the steward, rather than interpreting existing safe-method authorization as permission to publish questionable details.

Pass **privacy/rights clearance before challenge/grader qualification**. Stranger review and export guards assess sensitivity; discriminating correct/incorrect artifacts and calibrated model/provider trials assess evaluative value. Neither gate substitutes for the other. A new task need not reproduce the original model's failure; it must measure the preserved decision problem fairly.

Proposed generic families: visual placement despite correct arithmetic; shared mutable schema across provider handoffs; vacuous test-selection receipts; privacy over-removal versus residual secrets; provenance-based spoof-resistant classification; observation-window uncertainty; quantitative claims with wrong units/omitted classes and rendering faults. Roles are candidate mappings until actual execution establishes exposure. Keep any protected provenance private and cluster related mechanisms; task variants are not independent observations.

For each family, author a new specification, input generator, dev fixture and independent hidden grader reference. Check a discriminating good/bad pair and malformed/timeout output. Calibrate observed task hardness; do not require a chosen weak model to fail at an invented rate. Hold out generator variants/families; keep secret/reference answers grader-side. Provisional cancellation/serialization/recovery seeds need reproduced proof before admission. Writing/creative/image generation need dedicated tasks, not proxies from computer use or visual multiple choice.

Public source manifests should hold canonical repository/data URL, exact code/data revisions, task selection, license/access/asset conditions and adapter/metric deviations. Use fetch-pinned assets by default. Unresolved rights or data hashes remain blocked, not guessed. Acquisition isolation matters for remote code, pickled tests, downloaded images and oracle leakage. Never publish canary-protected/plaintext benchmark answers merely because code has a permissive license.

## 5. Repository versus local artifacts

**Safe source classes, after review:** public task specifications, newly generated synthetic dev fixtures and fake identities; generator/grader/schema versions and conformance pairs; adapters and public acquisition manifests; env-variable names without values; public code/data revisions; safe summary schema; approved export examples and recommendation rationale.

**Private by default:** raw captures/logs/prompts/responses/screenshots; personal host/user/path/endpoint/session/account/connection identifiers; exact private input or instruction hashes; credentials and credential-derived config hashes; source mappings; private heldout fixtures/reference keys; sensitive run plans and artifacts. A public author alias is permissible only for necessary attribution to an explicit public issue/PR/commit; it does not license associated private mail/account information.

Raw evaluation output goes to an explicit private, ignored workspace-root directory outside source repositories. No deletion of source-server records is implied. Retention/deletion policy requires its own authority. Separate acquisition, subject and grading environments; no personal home/browser/key-store mounts. Restrict credentials and network egress; disable framework telemetry/automatic public uploads; deny unmetered model calls from generated applications in causal tests. Hidden references must not be seeded for the solver.

Export is a separate gate: schema allowlist → generic private/secret-shape scan → local derived identity check → small-cell/unique-example review → fresh-context stranger read → fail-closed release/CI/dashboard upload. A static deny-list containing actual identities leaks those identities itself. Redaction regexes alone do not prove absence of PII. Public fixture task IDs may be exported under their source terms; private-origin task/connection/envelope IDs are never exported.

## 6. Adapter acceptance before live reuse

Build on existing evaluation bricks, not a duplicate general platform. The consumer orchestrator must capture the actual request/config/usage tree, classify subject versus controller/environment faults with receipts, keep artifacts before teardown where feasible, and separate functional execution from blinded judging. Existing completed-state summaries or free-text AIUser conclusions do not satisfy this contract.

Qualification cases: unique/repeated call IDs; descendants/retries/partial responses; missing traces/grades; changed source/fixture/model identity; preserved output with a regrade; accounting correction versus new spend; timeout/cancellation uncertainty; external application bypass; asymmetric missingness; saturated/ambiguous grader; secret/identity export rejection; cleanup failure with correct outcome retention. New adapters must verify preview/execution post-clamp semantics before supporting preset-bearing or backend/whole-policy experiments.

Enforce an approved global lifecycle/token/spend policy with explicit unknown-cost handling. DTU concurrency is not total call concurrency; routing preset limit names may be inert. Register every external resource immediately and reconcile confirmed teardown/absence. No scheduler or resource is created by these documents.
